# Rules for jobs Univapay Public API runs on its own

## authorization-expiry

- **silent**, state/side-effects: From 1 January 2027 (announced as planned), an authorization stays uncaptured for 25 days → The authorization is cancelled automatically. **Do:** Capture every authorization within 25 days, or cancel it and authorize again before the 25 days run out. *(announcement `content/ja/docs/changelog/2027-2.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/first/billing-type.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/api/charges/overview.md`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `background.39`)*

## direct-debit-schedule

- **silent**, -/side-effects: A debit cycle's upload deadline passes → The cycle's transfers change from `unlocked` to `locked` and can no longer be changed or deleted. **Do:** Finish all changes to a cycle's transfers before its upload deadline. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/direct-debit.md`; `background.3`)*
- **silent**, -/side-effects: A scheduled transfer is collected by the bank → It stays `awaiting` until the bank's result arrives, a few days after the debit date (on the result registration date), then becomes `successful` or `failed` with the bank's reason. **Do:** Poll the transfer from the result registration date onwards to get its result and failure reason. Also: `getDirectDebitBankTransfer`. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/direct-debit.md`; `background.4`)*
- **silent**, account-config/values: A transfer is collected in the merchant's debit cycle → Funds are debited on the 14th or the 27th per the merchant's `debit_date`; `calculated_debit_date` gives the actual business day. **Do:** Use calculated_debit_date, not the 14th or 27th itself, as the collection date. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/direct-debit.md`; `background.5`)*
- **silent**, order/side-effects: Transfers are scheduled on an account whose signed direct-debit request form has not been accepted by the bank (only the account data was registered) → The transfer fails; the definition's reason for a missing form is `no_account_transfer_request`. **Do:** Schedule transfers only for accounts whose signed request form has been sent and accepted. Also: `createDirectDebitBankTransfer`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/direct-debit.md`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `background.6`)*
- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: The merchant has direct-debit notification emails switched on (see the notification configuration) → Emails go 2 business days before the form-arrival deadline, 2 business days before and on the upload deadline, and on the result registration date. **Do:** Don't build your own reminders on the assumption that no emails are sent; check the notification configuration. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/direct-debit.md`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `background.7`)*

## payment-completion

- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: The customer pays a bank transfer, and the amount is exact, short or more than due, and the matching email notification is enabled → UnivaPay emails the deposit-received, short-payment or over-payment notice. **Do:** Do not send a duplicate email for this event unless the matching notification is switched off in the console (General settings > Notifications). *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/mail/template.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/mail.md`; `background.44`)*
- **silent**, -/webhooks: A konbini, bank-transfer or online-wallet charge reaches its final status because the consumer paid (or the deadline passed) → The charge_finished webhook is sent. **Do:** Update the order from charge_finished (or GET charge) for konbini, bank-transfer and online-wallet payments. *(docs `content/tested/sandbox-api-behaviour.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/individual/cvs/api.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/individual/bank/api.md`; `background.48`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: In test mode, a konbini charge is applied for → The charge is awaiting (applied for but not yet paid) right after the application and becomes successful automatically about 10 minutes later. **Do:** In test mode, treat a konbini charge as awaiting after the application, and wait about 10 minutes (or for charge_finished) before expecting it to succeed. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/individual/cvs/feature.md`; docs `content/tested/sandbox-api-behaviour.md`; `background.50`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A bank-transfer charge receives a deposit equal to or larger than the charge amount → The charge becomes successful; an excess is also treated as successful, and because bank transfer has no refund function the merchant must return the excess outside UnivaPay. **Do:** Check charged_amount against the order and return any excess to the consumer yourself. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/individual/bank/feature.md`; `background.52`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A bank-transfer charge receives less than the charge amount before its deadline → The charge stays awaiting (pending payment, short deposit) and further deposits to the same account are accepted until the deadline. **Do:** Keep the order open while a bank-transfer charge is short-paid; listen for the bank_transfer_status_updated webhook if needed. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/individual/bank/feature.md`; `background.53`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A bank-transfer recurring token (fixed account number) has several awaiting charges and a deposit arrives → The deposit is applied to the oldest charge first; the account stops accepting deposits only when all its charges are successful or failed. **Do:** Expect deposits to settle the oldest open charge on a recurring bank-transfer token first. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/individual/bank/feature.md`; `background.55`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A deposit to a bank-transfer charge's account is confirmed → The charge status and result are updated within about one hour, at any time or day. **Do:** Allow up to about an hour after a deposit before the charge status changes. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/individual/bank/feature.md`; `background.56`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: In test mode, an online-wallet charge is awaiting and its issuer token is requested → The charge stays awaiting until the issuer token is requested and becomes successful about two seconds after the request. **Do:** In test mode, request the issuer token to complete an online-wallet charge, then poll for successful. Also: `getChargeIssuerToken`. *(docs `content/tested/sandbox-api-behaviour.md`; `getChargeIssuerToken.4`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: In test mode, a bank-transfer charge created through the API is awaiting and its issuer token is fetched → After the issuer token is fetched the charge becomes successful with an exact-amount result; expiry and overpayment cannot be tested. **Do:** Fetch the issuer token in test mode to complete a bank-transfer test charge. Also: `getChargeIssuerToken`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/individual/bank/feature.md`; `getChargeIssuerToken.5`)*

## payment-deadline

- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: A bank-transfer or konbini payment deadline passes without payment, and the matching email notification is enabled → UnivaPay emails the deadline-expired notice (for bank transfer, a reminder with a new deadline when that setting is on). **Do:** Do not send a duplicate email for this event unless the matching notification is switched off in the console (General settings > Notifications). *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/mail/template.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/mail.md`; `background.43`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: An online-wallet payment is not completed by the consumer within 5 minutes → The charge fails with payment error 327. **Do:** Show the wallet payment page promptly and create a new charge if the consumer took longer than 5 minutes. *(docs `content/ja/faqsite/article-000001009.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/api/error/overview.md`; `background.49`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A konbini charge is not paid by its payment deadline → The charge becomes failed and a payment-failure email is sent. **Do:** Treat a failed konbini charge as expired and create a new charge if the consumer still wants to pay. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/individual/cvs/feature.md`; `background.51`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A bank-transfer charge has not been fully paid when its deadline passes → The charge becomes failed and the account is stopped so no further deposits are possible; any partial deposit must be returned by the merchant. **Do:** Create a new charge if the consumer still wants to pay, and refund partial deposits outside UnivaPay. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/individual/bank/feature.md`; `background.54`)*
- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: A payment fails, or a konbini payment's deadline passes, and the matching email notification is enabled → UnivaPay emails the payment-failure notice to the email address used for the payment. **Do:** Do not send a duplicate email for this event unless the matching notification is switched off in the console (General settings > Notifications). Also: `createCharge`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/mail/template.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/mail.md`; `createCharge.16`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A charge that requires 3-D Secure (awaiting) is not authenticated by the consumer within 5 minutes; in test mode such a charge was seen to fail without further action → The charge fails (payment error 327: payment deadline, 5-minute timeout, has passed). **Do:** Have the consumer complete 3-D Secure within 5 minutes of creating the charge, and create a new charge if it fails. Also: `createCharge`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/3ds/default-rules.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/api/error/overview.md`; docs `content/tested/sandbox-api-behaviour.md`; docs `content/ja/faqsite/article-000001009.md`; `createCharge.46`)*

## scheduled-capture

- **silent**, -/side-effects: A subscription is created with first_charge_authorization_only true and first_charge_capture_after set → The first charge is only authorized and is captured automatically after the given number of days. **Do:** Expect the first subscription charge to stay authorized until the automatic capture, and cancel it before then if the order is dropped. Also: `createSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/api/subscriptions/request/create.md`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `background.59`)*
- **silent**, -/side-effects: A card charge is created with capture false and capture_at → The authorization is captured automatically at the capture_at date and time. **Do:** Set capture_at only when the authorization should be captured automatically; otherwise capture it yourself. Also: `createCharge`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/api/charges/overview.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/api/charges/request/create.md`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `createCharge.23`)*

## subscription-billing

- **loud**, account-config/-: A subscription's per-charge amount is above the maximum charge amount set in the contract → outcome not stated. **Do:** Keep each subscription charge at or below the account's maximum charge amount. Also: `createSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/subs.md`; `createSubscription.49`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A subscription is due on a billing day → Charges start running from 07:00 on the billing day, or from 09:00 for daily subscriptions and when the start date is one day after the subscription was created. **Do:** Do not expect scheduled subscription charges (and their webhooks) before 07:00 (09:00 in those cases) on the billing day. *(docs `content/ja/docs/migration/specification.md`; `background.40`)*
- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: A subscription's second or later payment succeeds or fails, and the matching email notification is enabled → UnivaPay sends the subscription-payment success or failure notification email. **Do:** Do not send a duplicate email for this event unless the matching notification is switched off in the console (General settings > Notifications). *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/mail/template.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/mail.md`; `background.45`)*
- **silent**, -/other: UnivaPay runs a subscription's scheduled charge on its own → The result is available only through webhooks or GET/LIST requests (no response to the app). **Do:** Subscribe to subscription webhooks or poll the subscription's charges to learn each scheduled payment's result. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/first/notification_and_retrieval.md`; `background.46`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A subscription creates its charges → The metadata set on the subscription is carried over to the charges it creates. **Do:** Put order references on the subscription metadata to find them on its charges. *(docs `content/ja/docs/api/charges/overview.md`; `background.57`)*
- **silent**, order/side-effects: The subscription's first charge is an authorization only (first_charge_authorization_only true) → The billing cycle starts only after the first charge is captured. **Do:** Capture the first charge (or let it auto-capture) before expecting the subscription's recurring charges to start. Also: `createSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription.md`; `background.60`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A subscription has subscription_plan.plan_type fixed_cycles → The first charge counts as one of the fixed number of payments. **Do:** Count the first charge when choosing fixed_cycles. Also: `createSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/migration/specification.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/implement/widget/param-subscription.md`; `background.61`)*
- **silent**, -/side-effects: A retry succeeds after one or more later scheduled payment dates have passed → The missed scheduled charges are run one day apart, and once they succeed billing returns to the original schedule. **Do:** Expect several charges on consecutive days after a late successful retry, and tell the customer. Also: `background:subscription-retry`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/retry.md`; `background.71`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A fixed-count subscription makes its last payment, or an installment-plan subscription's payment succeeds → The status becomes completed. **Do:** Treat completed as the normal end of a fixed-count or installment subscription. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/api/subscriptions/overview.md`; docs `content/ja/faqsite/article-000001072.md`; `background.73`)*
- **silent**, -/other: A subscription's later cyclic charges run (every charge after the first) → 3-D Secure is not run on them; it runs only on the subscription's first charge. **Do:** Complete 3-D Secure on the subscription's first charge, and do not expect the consumer to authenticate again for the later cyclic charges. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/3ds/default-rules.md`; `background.74`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A subscription's scheduled charges run → Charges start from 07:00 on the charge date in the zone_id time zone, or from 09:00 for daily subscriptions and when start_on is one day after the creation date. **Do:** Set zone_id to the customer's time zone and expect scheduled charges from 07:00 (09:00 in the two listed cases), not at midnight. Also: `createSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/api/subscriptions/request/create.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/api/subscriptions/request/payment.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/migration/specification.md`; `createSubscription.44`)*
- **silent**, -/values: schedule_settings.preserve_end_of_month is true, period is monthly and start_on is the last day of a month → Every later charge falls on the last day of its month (e.g. 2018-06-30 then 2018-07-31; with false, 2018-07-30). **Do:** Set preserve_end_of_month true for month-end billing with a month-end start_on. Also: `createSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/api/subscriptions/request/create.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/implement/widget/param-subscription.md`; `createSubscription.45`)*
- **silent**, state/values: A suspended subscription is resumed after its next payment date has passed → Nothing is charged right away; the next payment date is set to the nearest future cycle date, and missed past payments are not charged. **Do:** Read next_payment.due_date after resuming and tell the customer when the next charge is. Also: `unsuspendSubscription`, `updateSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/stop-resume.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/subs.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/migration/specification.md`; `unsuspendSubscription.4`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: A suspended subscription is resumed on the same day as its next payment date → It is charged right after the resume, unless a charge already ran that day. **Do:** Expect an immediate charge when resuming on the billing day. Also: `unsuspendSubscription`, `updateSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/stop-resume.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/subs.md`; `unsuspendSubscription.5`)*
- **silent**, state/values: A subscription with a fixed number of payments is resumed while past payments are unpaid → The missed payments are added at the end of the schedule, so the last payment date moves later. **Do:** Show the customer the extended schedule after resuming a fixed-count subscription. Also: `unsuspendSubscription`, `updateSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/stop-resume.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/subs.md`; `unsuspendSubscription.6`)*
- **silent**, -/side-effects: next_payment.terminate_with_status is set to suspended or canceled → On the next payment execution date (a retry date included) the subscription is suspended or canceled instead of being charged. **Do:** Use terminate_with_status to schedule a stop at the next payment instead of stopping the subscription at once. Also: `updateSubscription`. *(docs `content/tested/sandbox-api-behaviour.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/api/subscriptions/request/update.md`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/stop-resume.md`; `updateSubscription.11`)*
- **silent**, -/values: The next payment date is changed (the source describes the change in the console) → Later payment dates are counted from the new date, not from the original cycle day. **Do:** After moving a next payment date, move it back once that payment succeeds if the original billing day must be kept. Also: `updateSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/faqsite/article-000001066.md`; `updateSubscription.18`)*
- **silent**, -/side-effects: The next payment date is changed to today (the source describes the change in the console) → The charge runs within about 20 minutes, unless a charge already ran that day (successful or not), in which case nothing is charged. **Do:** Set the next payment date to tomorrow or later when a charge already ran today. Also: `updateSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/faqsite/article-000001066.md`; `updateSubscription.19`)*

## subscription-retry

- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: A subscription's charges keep failing and `subscription_configuration.failed_charges_to_cancel` is set → The subscription is cancelled once that number of failed charges is exceeded. **Do:** Read failed_charges_to_cancel from GET /me and act on failed subscription payments before the limit is reached. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `background.1`)*
- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: An installment plan's charges keep failing and `installments_configuration.failed_cycles_to_cancel` is set → The installment plan is cancelled automatically after that number of failed charges. **Do:** Watch installment payment failures and expect automatic cancellation at the configured count. *(docs `content/ja/docs/api/merchant/overview.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/api/store/overview.md`; `background.2`)*
- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: A subscription payment has failed as many times as the retry count set in the console → The subscription's status changes to the status chosen in the console for after the retries end (e.g. `suspended`, which sends `subscription_suspended`). **Do:** Watch for `subscription_suspended` (or the configured end status) after failed retries and ask the customer to update the payment method. *(docs `content/ja/files/link.upc-app.com/gpp_console-guide.pdf.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/webhook.md`; `background.47`)*
- **silent**, account-config/values: A subscription payment fails and is retried → The number of attempts is the console retry count, which includes the first attempt (default 3, so 2 actual retries). **Do:** Check the retry count in the console settings and count the first attempt in it. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/retry.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/subs.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/migration/specification.md`; docs `content/ja/faqsite/article-000001074.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/migration/parameters.md`; `background.62`)*
- **silent**, account-config/side-effects: Every attempt up to the configured retry count fails → The subscription stops at once with the console's end status: suspended by default, or canceled if so configured. **Do:** Check the end-of-retry status setting and handle subscription_suspended or subscription_canceled after failed payments. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/retry.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/subs.md`; docs `content/ja/faqsite/article-000001074.md`; `background.63`)*
- **silent**, state/side-effects: The retry count is reached while a stop is scheduled for the next payment date (termination settings) → The subscription stops when the last retry fails, whatever the scheduled-stop setting says. **Do:** Do not rely on a scheduled stop date when payments are failing. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/stop-resume.md`; `background.64`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A subscription has no retry_interval set → retry_interval stays null, and retries use the cycle length divided by the retry count, rounded down to whole days, with a monthly cycle counted as 30 days; a set retry_interval takes precedence. **Do:** Set retry_interval explicitly when the derived interval (e.g. 30 / 4 = 7 days) does not suit the business. Also: `createSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/retry.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/subs.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/implement/link/param-sabs_n_inst.md`; docs `content/tested/sandbox-api-behaviour.md`; announcement `content/ja/docs/changelog/2023-2.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/migration/specification.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/migration/parameters.md`; `background.65`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A subscription payment fails → The status becomes unpaid and the retry interval is counted from the day of the failed attempt. **Do:** Show unpaid subscriptions as awaiting retry and expect the retry one interval after the failure day. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/retry.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/subs.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/api/subscriptions/overview.md`; docs `content/ja/faqsite/article-000001147.md`; `background.66`)*
- **silent**, state/values: A subscription is waiting for a retry (unpaid) and has a next payment date → The retry runs on the later of the failure date plus the retry interval and the next payment date, so moving the next payment date earlier does not bring the retry forward. **Do:** Shorten retry_interval rather than the next payment date to retry an unpaid subscription sooner. Also: `updateSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/retry.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/migration/specification.md`; docs `content/ja/faqsite/article-000001147.md`; docs `content/ja/faqsite/article-000001073.md`; `background.67`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A retry attempt fails → The next retry cannot be on the same day; the earliest is the next day. **Do:** Do not expect a second retry on the day of a failed retry. *(docs `content/ja/faqsite/article-000001073.md`; `background.68`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A retry succeeds → The retry count is reset, so the next failed payment again gets the full configured number of retries. **Do:** Count retries per failed payment, not over the life of the subscription. *(docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/subs.md`; announcement `content/ja/docs/changelog/2024-2.md`; `background.69`)*
- **silent**, -/values: A retry succeeds before the next scheduled payment date → Billing returns to the original schedule. **Do:** Expect the next charge on the original cycle date after an early successful retry. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/retry.md`; `background.70`)*
- **silent**, -/side-effects: A retry succeeds after one or more later scheduled payment dates have passed → The missed scheduled charges are run one day apart, and once they succeed billing returns to the original schedule. **Do:** Expect several charges on consecutive days after a late successful retry, and tell the customer. Also: `background:subscription-billing`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/retry.md`; `background.71`)*
- **silent**, state/values: A subscription suspended after failed retries is resumed → The full configured number of retries is run again. **Do:** Update the card before resuming a subscription that was suspended for failed payments. Also: `unsuspendSubscription`, `updateSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/retry.md`; `background.72`)*
- **silent**, state/values: A current subscription that was suspended on request is resumed → Even though it shows unpaid, no retry date is set and it is charged on the next payment date. **Do:** Use next_payment.due_date, not retry_date, as the next charge date after resuming a manually suspended subscription. Also: `unsuspendSubscription`, `updateSubscription`. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/subscription/retry.md`; `unsuspendSubscription.7`)*

## test-data-deletion

- **silent**, other/side-effects: Test-mode charges, subscriptions and recurring tokens are older than 180 days (since the 2026-06-08 change) → They are deleted together with their tokens, charges and refunds; charges on a deleted test recurring token can no longer be refunded. **Do:** Do not keep test-mode ids for longer than 180 days, and recreate test data when it is needed again. *(announcement `content/ja/docs/changelog/2026-2.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/test.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/first/verification.md`; `background.38`)*

## webhook-delivery

- **silent**, -/webhooks: An event fires for a webhook that subscribes to it → The event is sent to the webhook's URL as an HTTP POST with `Content-Type: application/json` and `User-Agent: UnivaPay Webhook Agent`. **Do:** Expose a publicly reachable endpoint that accepts JSON POST requests for the webhook. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/webhook.md`; docs `content/tested/sandbox-api-behaviour.md`; `background.8`)*
- **silent**, auth/webhooks: A webhook has an `auth_token` set → Each delivery's `Authorization` header is exactly the `auth_token` value, with no `Bearer ` prefix; no signature header is sent. **Do:** Authenticate deliveries by comparing the whole `Authorization` header with the stored `auth_token` (do not expect `Bearer ` or a signature). Also: `createWebhook`, `updateWebhook`. **Conflict:** The definition calls auth_token a bearer token; the tested page (2026-11-23) saw the raw value with no Bearer prefix. The record follows the tested page. *(docs `content/tested/sandbox-api-behaviour.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/webhook.md`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; docs `content/ja/docs/api/webhooks/overview.md`; `background.9`)*
- **silent**, -/webhooks: A webhook delivery is sent → The JSON body has only two top-level keys, `event` and `data`; there is no delivery `id` or `created_on`. **Do:** Deduplicate deliveries with `event` plus the ids and status inside `data`, not with a delivery id. **Conflict:** The definition's webhook envelope has `id` and `created_on`; the tested page saw only `event` and `data`. The record follows the tested page. *(docs `content/tested/sandbox-api-behaviour.md`; spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `background.10`)*
- **silent**, resilience/webhooks: The receiving endpoint does not answer a delivery with 200 within 3 seconds (tested: an answer after 6 seconds) → UnivaPay treats the delivery as failed (timeout) and delivers it again, even if the endpoint did receive it. **Do:** Answer 200 within 3 seconds and do the processing after replying (asynchronously). *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/webhook.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/webhook.md`; docs `content/tested/sandbox-api-behaviour.md`; `background.11`)*
- **silent**, resilience/webhooks: A webhook delivery fails and is retried → Retries come 1 minute after the failure and then at growing intervals (1, 2, 4, 8 minutes, ...) up to at most 15 minutes apart. **Do:** Expect the same event to arrive again minutes later, and keep the endpoint idempotent. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/webhook.md`; docs `content/tested/sandbox-api-behaviour.md`; `background.12`)*
- **silent**, -/webhooks: The receiving endpoint answers a delivery with a 2xx status (within 3 seconds) → The delivery counts as successful and is not retried. **Do:** Reply with a 2xx status once the delivery is received. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/webhook.md`; `background.13`)*
- **silent**, resilience/webhooks: The receiving endpoint answers a delivery with a 3xx status → The delivery is not retried and the webhook is stopped immediately. **Do:** Never answer webhook deliveries with a redirect; point the webhook at the final URL. *(docs `content/ja/docs/guide/detail/webhook.md`; docs `content/ja/docs/faq/individual/webhook.md`; `background.14`)*
