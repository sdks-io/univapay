- **silent**, -/values: A transfer is scheduled → It is placed in the merchant's next debit cycle and can be edited until that cycle's upload deadline. **Do:** Check the current schedule's upload deadline to know which month a new transfer falls in. *(spec `spec/openapi.yaml`; `createDirectDebitBankTransfer.3`)*
